# How to Play Breed Rush

Breed Rush is a team dog-breed quiz. A team sees a photo of a dog and picks the correct breed from four multiple-choice answers before a countdown runs out. Teams take turns, and the team with the most points at the end wins.

## Setting up a game

The setup screen is a three-step wizard.

1. **Number of questions.** Choose 5, 10, 15 or 20. The cards are called Quick Sniff (5), Good Boy (10), Top Dog (15) and Alpha (20). Each card shows a rough time per team: about 1 minute for 5 questions, 2 minutes for 10, 3 minutes for 15 and 4 minutes for 20.
2. **Number of teams.** Choose 1 to 4. One team is called Solo, two is a Duel, three is a Trio and four is a Pack. The maximum is four teams.
3. **Team names.** Every team needs a name of up to 20 characters. Names must be different from each other. The "Surprise me with names" button fills in fun suggestions such as Good Boys, Top Dogs or Paw Patrol.

When all names are valid, press "Start the showdown".

## Taking turns

Teams play one after another on the same screen. Every team gets exactly the same questions in the same order, so the contest is fair. Before each team starts, a "you're up" screen shows the team name, how many dogs are in the round, the time limit and the points per question. Press "Let's go" when the team is ready.

## Answering a question

Each question shows one dog photo and four breed names labelled A, B, C and D. Click an answer or press the matching key. After you answer, the right answer turns green and a wrong pick turns red, and a message tells you how many points you earned. Press "Next dog" or the Enter key to continue.

## The timer

Every question has a 15 second countdown shown as a ring on the photo. The ring changes colour as time runs low and the numbers tick faster in the last five seconds. The clock only starts once the photo has finished loading, so a slow picture never costs you time. If the time runs out, the question counts as wrong and the team is charged the full 15 seconds.

## Pausing

Press the Pause button, or the P or Escape key, to stop the clock. While the game is paused the photo and the answer choices are hidden, so nobody can study them. Paused time is never counted against a team. Switching to another browser tab pauses the game automatically. From the pause screen you can resume or quit to the menu. Quitting loses the progress of that game.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **A, B, C, D** (or **1, 2, 3, 4**): choose an answer.
- **H**: use a hint (this costs points, see the scoring guide).
- **P** or **Escape**: pause and resume.
- **Enter** or **Space**: continue after an answer.

## When the game ends

After the last team finishes the last question, the results screen shows an animated podium, the winner and a table with points, correct answers, hints used and time for every team. The scores are saved to the leaderboard automatically. Scores are only saved when a game reaches the results screen, so quitting from the pause menu or closing the browser early does not record anything.
