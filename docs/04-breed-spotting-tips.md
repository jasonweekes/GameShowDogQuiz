# How to Tell Dog Breeds Apart

Want to get better at Breed Rush? Learn to read a dog's features. Look at the whole dog first, then at the details.

## Start with the big picture

1. **Size and build.** Is the dog tiny, medium or giant? Is the body lean and tall like a sight hound, or stocky and muscular like a bulldog?
2. **Coat.** Smooth and short, long and silky, wiry, curly, double-coated and fluffy, or corded?
3. **Colour and markings.** Look for a mask, a white blaze, spots, a saddle pattern or merle (a marbled coat).
4. **Head and muzzle.** A long narrow muzzle suggests a sight hound or collie. A flat, short face (brachycephalic) suggests a Pug, Boxer, Bulldog or Pekingese.
5. **Ears and tail.** These are often the quickest clue.

## Ear shapes

- **Prick ears** stand straight up: German Shepherd, Siberian Husky, Samoyed, Chihuahua, Pembroke Welsh Corgi.
- **Drop (hanging) ears** fold down: Beagle, Labrador Retriever, Golden Retriever, Basset Hound.
- **Rose ears** fold back and show the inside of the ear: Greyhound, Whippet, Italian Greyhound.
- **Button ears** fold forward at the tip: Fox Terrier, Russell Terrier, Border Terrier.
- **Bat ears** are large and upright: French Bulldog, Papillon (the butterfly-like ears), Chihuahua.
- **Long, feathered ears** hang low with silky hair: Cocker Spaniel, Irish Setter, Basset Hound.

## Tail clues

A tightly curled tail over the back suggests northern or spitz breeds such as the Akita, Samoyed, Husky, Pomeranian and Shiba Inu. A long, thin, whip-like tail points to a Greyhound or Whippet. A thick, otter-like tail suggests a Labrador. A plume of feathered tail suits a setter or a Collie. Some breeds, such as the Pembroke Welsh Corgi, are often seen with a very short tail.

## Commonly confused look-alikes

- **German Shepherd, Belgian Malinois, Tervuren and Groenendael.** The German Shepherd is heavier, with a sloping back and a black saddle on tan. The Malinois is leaner and lighter, with a short fawn coat and a black mask. The Tervuren is a Belgian shepherd with a long coat in fawn with a black overlay. The Groenendael is the long-haired, all-black Belgian shepherd.
- **Husky, Alaskan Malamute, Samoyed and Eskimo dog.** The Siberian Husky is medium sized with a fox-like face and often blue eyes. The Alaskan Malamute is bigger and heavier, with a broader head and brown eyes. The Samoyed is all white or cream and famous for its "Sammy smile". The American Eskimo dog is a small white spitz.
- **Greyhound, Whippet, Italian Greyhound and Saluki.** The Greyhound is the largest and most muscular. The Whippet looks like a smaller Greyhound. The Italian Greyhound is a tiny toy version. The Saluki has long feathering on its ears and tail.
- **Border Collie, Rough Collie and Shetland Sheepdog.** The Border Collie has a medium length coat, often black and white, with a working-dog build. The Rough Collie ("Lassie") is large with a lush ruff. The Shetland Sheepdog looks like a small Rough Collie.
- **Pembroke and Cardigan Welsh Corgi.** The Pembroke has pointed upright ears and a very short or docked tail. The Cardigan is a little longer, has rounder, bigger ears and a long, fox-like tail.
- **Poodle sizes.** Standard, Miniature and Toy Poodles look alike and differ mainly in size. The Standard Poodle is the tallest, around 38 centimetres (15 inches) or more at the shoulder.
- **Schnauzer sizes.** Miniature and Giant Schnauzers share the bearded face and wiry coat but differ greatly in size.
- **Setters.** The Irish Setter is solid mahogany red. The Gordon Setter is black and tan. The English Setter has a flecked, speckled coat called belton.
- **Spaniels.** The English Springer Spaniel is a taller, white-and-liver or black-and-white working dog. The Cocker Spaniel is smaller with long ears. The Brittany is a leggy, almost tailless orange-and-white spaniel. The Sussex Spaniel has a golden-liver coat.
- **Mastiff types.** The English Mastiff is the largest and heaviest. The Bullmastiff is a bit smaller with a short fawn or brindle coat. The Tibetan Mastiff has a thick lion-like mane.
- **Labrador and Golden Retriever.** The Labrador has a short, dense coat and an otter tail. The Golden Retriever has a longer, feathered golden coat.
- **Bulldogs.** The English Bulldog has rose ears and a heavy wrinkled face. The French Bulldog has upright bat ears. The Boston Bulldog and Boston Terrier are both compact, but the Boston Terrier has a tuxedo-like black and white coat.

## Handy quiz strategy

Use elimination. Read all four answers first, look at the dog and rule out any breed that is clearly the wrong size or coat. If two answers are look-alikes, look for the one small clue such as ear shape or tail. Hints cost points, so try to use them only when you really need to.
