# Companion Dogs for Every Mood and Situation

Dogs have a special talent for making hard moments feel lighter. Tiffee uses this guide when someone asks for something she is not able to help with, such as homework, maths problems, coding, essays or general chores. Instead of doing the task, she suggests a breed that would make a comforting companion for the situation. This is friendly, general advice about dog personalities. Every dog is an individual, and a real dog is a long commitment of time, money and care, so think carefully and consider adopting from a shelter or rescue.

## Stressed about homework or exams

A calm, patient dog is the perfect study buddy. The **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel** is gentle and affectionate and loves nothing more than curling up beside you while you work. The **Greyhound** is nicknamed the "40 mile an hour couch potato" because it sprints briefly and then sleeps most of the day, so it will happily nap next to your desk. The **Golden Retriever** is gentle, patient and famous for calming people down. The **Newfoundland** is a huge, sweet-natured dog that is happy to rest its big head on your lap. A study break with a short walk or a game of fetch is also a proven way to clear your head.

## Feeling sad, down or lonely

Affectionate dogs that love to stay close are wonderful for low moods. The **Labrador Retriever** is friendly and loyal and is one of the most common therapy and assistance dogs. The **Golden Retriever** is warm and loving. The **Havanese** is nicknamed a "velcro dog" because it follows its person everywhere. The **Bichon Frise** is cheerful and playful, and the **Maltese** and **Shih Tzu** are gentle lap dogs that love cuddles. The **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel** is so people-loving that it is often called a "comforter spaniel".

## Feeling anxious, nervous or overwhelmed

Calm, steady breeds help many people relax. The **Newfoundland**, **Saint Bernard** and **Bernese Mountain Dog** are gentle giants with soothing, patient temperaments. The **Basset Hound** is laid back and mellow. The **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel** is sweet and adaptable. The **Poodle** is intelligent, sheds very little and is widely used as a therapy dog. Stroking a calm dog and walking outside are both known to help many people feel less tense.

## Feeling tired and unmotivated

A cheerful, active dog gets you moving. The **Beagle** is merry and loves a good walk and sniff outdoors. The **Labrador Retriever** is always ready for fetch. The **Vizsla** is an energetic, affectionate "velcro" sporting dog that loves exercise. The **Australian Shepherd** and **Border Collie** are very energetic and need a lot of activity, so they suit people who want a running or hiking partner. A daily walk with a dog is an easy way to build a healthy routine.

## Bored and in need of a laugh

Some breeds are natural clowns. The **Pug** is a charming, comical companion. The **French Bulldog** and **Boston Terrier** are playful, funny little characters. The **Pembroke Welsh Corgi** is bouncy and full of personality. The **Dachshund** is bold and cheeky and loves to burrow under blankets. The **Beagle** has a famous howl that makes people laugh.

## Need big, warm hugs

For comforting cuddles, choose a big, gentle breed. The **Newfoundland**, **Saint Bernard**, **Bernese Mountain Dog**, **Great Dane** and **Leonberger** are gentle giants that love being close to their people. Remember that big dogs need more space, food and veterinary costs.

## Need help staying focused and organized

Smart, eager-to-please dogs make good partners for people who like routines and goals. The **Border Collie** is considered one of the most trainable breeds. The **Poodle** and **German Shepherd** are intelligent and love having a job. Teaching a dog tricks is a fun way to practise patience and consistency.

## Living in a small home or apartment

Calm, smaller breeds usually do best in small spaces. The **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel**, **Greyhound** (surprisingly quiet and relaxed indoors), **Shih Tzu**, **Maltese**, **Havanese** and **French Bulldog** are popular apartment dogs. Even small dogs still need daily walks and company.

## Families with children

Friendly, sturdy and patient breeds suit families. The **Labrador Retriever**, **Golden Retriever**, **Beagle**, **Collie** and **Newfoundland** are known for their gentleness with children. The Newfoundland has a reputation as a "nanny dog". Young children should always be supervised with any dog.

## Allergies and low-shedding dogs

No dog is completely free of allergens, but some breeds shed very little and are often easier for people with mild allergies. These include the **Poodle**, **Bichon Frise**, **Maltese**, **Havanese**, **Miniature Schnauzer** and **Yorkshire Terrier**. People with allergies should spend time with a breed before committing.

## A good all-round companion

If you are not sure which breed to pick, the **Golden Retriever**, **Labrador Retriever** and **Cavalier King Charles Spaniel** are three of the most popular, friendly and adaptable companions, with gentle temperaments that suit many different people.

## When something is more serious than a bad day

A dog can brighten a hard day, but it is not a replacement for help. If someone is feeling very low for a long time, feels unsafe, or is worried about hurting themselves, they should talk to a trusted adult, a doctor, or a local helpline or emergency service straight away.
